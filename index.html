Lotus U Yoga began with one belief: wellness should fit a real life, not the other way round. Varsha Patil — a Rishikesh-certified yoga trainer and Zumba instructor based in Indore — teaches in small batches so every posture, every breath and every adjustment actually reaches you. Come for the strength, stay for the calm.

Yoga Sessions

Group classes in Hatha, Vinyasa and Yin — sixty minutes of movement, breath and a long, honest stretch. Every class offers a beginner option, and the teacher actually watches your alignment. Studio sessions in Indore, live sessions online.

Yoga Therapy

One-to-one therapeutic yoga for back and neck pain, posture, PCOS, disturbed sleep and everyday stress. We begin with an assessment, then build a short daily practice you can genuinely keep — with progress checks as your body changes.
Enquire

Weight Management

A steady blend of yoga, functional movement and practical nutrition guidance — built for real Indian kitchens and real schedules. No crash diets: weekly structure, measurable progress and a practice you will not want to quit.

Zumba Sessions
